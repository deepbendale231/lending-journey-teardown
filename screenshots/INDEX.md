# Screenshot index

Every screenshot in this folder is listed here, and every screenshot cited in the journey map, friction log or RBI checklist
exists. `make check` enforces both directions.

**Naming:** `NN_short_name.png` or `.jpg`, numbered in the order you saw the screens (`01_home.png`, `02_personal_loan.png`, ...).
**Privacy:** blur phone numbers, names, account numbers and anything that identifies a person before you save the file.
Do not capture a real application: stop at the gate and say so in [01_journey_map.md](01_journey_map.md).
